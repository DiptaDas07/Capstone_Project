Sleep Apnea

Breathing Interruptions During Sleep

Sleep apnea causes repeated pauses in breathing throughout the night. Common signs include loud snoring, morning headaches, and excessive daytime fatigue. Early detection significantly reduces long-term cardiovascular risks and improves daily quality of life.

🌙
Insomnia

Difficulty Falling or Staying Asleep

Insomnia affects concentration, emotional well-being, and physical health. Whether chronic or stress-related, it disrupts the body's natural recovery cycle. Recognizing it early opens the door to lifestyle changes and targeted support before it worsens.
